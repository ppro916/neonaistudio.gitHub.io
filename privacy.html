Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information AI Nexus × Neon AI Studio (“we”, “us”) handles when you visit neonaistudio.in or contact us, why we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

1. Who we are

AI Nexus × Neon AI Studio is a creative and technology studio based in Maharashtra, India, serving clients worldwide. We are the controller of the personal information described here. Contact us at moreshwar1234bhonde@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 9226520552.

2. What we collect

  • Details you send us. The quote form on our website does not store anything on our servers. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, your name, chosen service and project details are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. We only receive them if you send that message. If you email or call us, we receive what you choose to share (for example your name, phone number, email address, business details and files).
  • Information stored on your device. To prevent spam, the website keeps the time of your recent quote requests in your browser's local storage, and keeps your prepared WhatsApp message for the current tab only (session storage) so the “Open WhatsApp” button works on the thank-you page. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Analytics (only if switched on and only with your consent). We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are useful. If it is switched on, it runs only after you press “Allow” in the cookie banner, and never if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. It uses cookies such as _ga to count visits, pages viewed, approximate location (country/city), device and browser type. Advertising features are switched off.
  • Hosting and security logs. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers automatically process technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request, to deliver the site, keep it secure and block abuse.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and deliver the work you order.
  • To keep records of projects, invoices and payments where the law requires it.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent spam and fix problems.
  • If analytics is enabled and you consent, to understand and improve our pages.

Our legal bases (for visitors covered by the EU/UK GDPR) are: taking steps at your request before a contract and performing the contract; our legitimate interests in running and securing the website; your consent for analytics cookies; and compliance with legal obligations.

4. We do not sell your data

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client project files and messages: for the duration of the project and up to 3 years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Invoices and payment records: as long as tax law requires (in India, generally up to 8 years).
  • Analytics data (if enabled): 14 months, the shortest standard Google Analytics setting.
  • Data stored in your browser: anti-spam timestamps expire after 24 hours; the prepared WhatsApp message is cleared when you close the tab.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent at any time.

  • EU, EEA and UK (including Luxembourg): under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Luxembourg this is the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), and in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
  • United Arab Emirates: under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you may request access to, correction or erasure of your data, restrict or stop processing, and object to processing. Clients in the DIFC or ADGM also have rights under those regulations.
  • India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may request access, correction and erasure, and nominate another person to act for you.

To use any of these rights, email moreshwar1234bhonde@gmail.com or message +91 9226520552. We will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first.
